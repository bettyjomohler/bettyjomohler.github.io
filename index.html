About

I am an Applied Scientist in human-centered machine learning and extended reality. I am an expert in extended reality, wearable devices, self-avatars and human perception, with two decades of research and eight years as a Principal Applied Scientist in machine learning at the Amazon Research and Development Center in Tübingen, building multimodal training datasets for vision-language models, image and video generation, generative-AI benchmarks and personalized machine learning.

Before moving to industry, I held a tenured W2 professorship at TU Darmstadt and led a W2 Max Planck Minerva research group in Tübingen. I continue to have active clinical and school partnerships in Tübingen.

Research

Medium

Extended reality & wearables

VR, AR, smart glasses and body-worn sensors as the way people meet AI, and as precise instruments for measuring perception, movement and behavior.

Method

Human-centered machine learning

Multimodal behavioral data and human-in-the-loop benchmarks that show whether generative and multimodal models actually work for people.

Impact

Clinics & classrooms

3D body and motion models for stroke and eating-disorder assessment, and research on how AI and wearables shape students’ learning, fitness and sense of self.
